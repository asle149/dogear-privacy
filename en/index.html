Dogear Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Your library and reading records are stored on your device by default. You can optionally connect Dropbox to sync selected types of records and import books. The developer does not receive your books or records through these features.

Connecting Dropbox requests read and write access to all files. Dogear reads folder listings and the book list for the folder selected in the Dropbox tab. It also reads subfolder listings when “Include subfolders” is enabled or you use “Add books from this folder.” This includes file details such as names, sizes, and modification dates, and selected books are downloaded to your device. Dogear also reads and writes files needed for sync. All writes are restricted to the “Dogear Reader” folder at the top of your Dropbox.

When sync is enabled, Dogear automatically exchanges and merges reading positions, bookmarks, and highlights with notes for matching book files. Highlighted text, notes, colors, positions within the book, chapter titles, and record creation or update times are stored in per-book files under /Dogear Reader/sync/v1/books/ in your Dropbox. Dogear does not encrypt these files before storing them. Data is encrypted in transit.

Book notes, shelves, reading dates, reading time, and listening positions are not synced through Dropbox. This feature does not upload original book files. PDFs read after conversion to EPUB are excluded from sync.

Credentials used to maintain the connection and information identifying the connected Dropbox account are stored on your device. Connection credentials are encrypted using the device’s security features and excluded from app backups and Android automatic backups. Dogear communicates directly with Dropbox, without passing data through a developer server.

When you delete a highlight or bookmark, a deletion record remains in the sync file so the deletion can reach other devices. It contains an identifier and update and deletion times, rather than the deleted content. Deleting a book in the app or disconnecting Dropbox does not delete its sync files from Dropbox.

To delete Dogear data stored in Dropbox, tap “Disconnect” in Dogear on every connected device, delete the “Dogear Reader” folder in Dropbox, then disconnect Dogear under “Connected apps” in Dropbox settings. Records may be uploaded again if a device remains connected. Dropbox’s privacy policy applies to its processing of data and any retention after deletion.

Dogear contains no advertising networks or analytics tools. It does not send usage statistics or device identifiers to the developer.

Text recognition runs on your device. Images are not sent to an external server for recognition, and the original image is not kept in the app after recognition.

When you share a book link to fill in its title and cover, or paste a book page link on the book screen to get its cover, the app visits that address and reads the publicly available information. The site’s privacy policy applies to this connection.

When you select a word in the viewer and open the dictionary, the selected word is sent to Naver Dictionary for a search. The full book and your reading records are not sent with it. Naver’s privacy policy applies to processing by Naver Dictionary.

You can export records and settings with the app’s backup feature. Exported backups are password-protected by default; if you forget the password, the developer cannot recover them. Uninstalling the app removes its local data, but exported backups and files in Dropbox must be deleted separately.

If Google backup is enabled on your device, reading records and settings may be backed up automatically to your Google account. Book files, images, and Dropbox connection credentials are excluded. The developer does not receive or access these backups. You can turn off Google backup in Android settings.

Dogear is not intended for children under 14.

Changes to this policy will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

When you search the web for a cover, the app sends the book title followed by “표지” (cover) to Google. Cookies and site data from Google are stored on your device in a space used only for search, separate from the viewer. The app gets the image you choose directly from the site that hosts it without sending login information. Google’s privacy policy applies to processing by Google.
